PREMOVE
Stockfish locks in its move
before you play yours
How It Works
The Core Rule
In normal chess, both players think after seeing the opponent's move. Here, Stockfish commits to its reply before you move — blind. Once you see the locked indicator light up in the opponent's bar, the engine's move is already decided. Whatever you play, it fires that move instantly.
How Stockfish Picks Its Premove
While you're thinking, the engine analyses the position and tries to find a move that works safely across the widest range of things you might play. It ranks your candidate moves by how likely they are, then for each one asks: what's my best reply here? The move that holds up well across the most weighted scenarios becomes the premove.

A blunder penalty discards moves that look good against the predicted line but hang a piece in an alternative — like a knight that's great on f6 until a pawn advances to attack it.
The Fallback Hierarchy
No premove is perfectly universal, so there are layers:
  1. 1 Conditional — a move currently illegal for the engine (e.g. a recapture on a square you might capture first). Fires only if your move makes it legal.
  2. 2 Generic — the main blind premove. The broadly-safe choice. Fires roughly 75% of the time.
  3. 3 Backup — a different piece from the generic, in case your move captured the generic piece.
  4. 4 Check response — a prepared reply to specific checks. Generic moves are usually illegal when in check, so these are stored separately.
  5. 5 Live search — all premoves failed or weren't computed in time. The engine thinks from scratch, searching to the same depth configured in the setup screen. This is the only case it gets to see your move first — but it's still bound by the chosen depth, not unlimited thinking time.
The Indicator
The dot in the top-right of the header shows engine state.
Pulsing orange — computing premoves or searching.
Solid green — premove is locked and ready.

The locked label in the opponent bar lights up the moment a safe generic premove is confirmed. If it's still dark when you move, the engine will fall through the hierarchy above.
Strategy
Stockfish handicaps itself by committing blind. You can exploit this: play surprising moves that the engine didn't anticipate, create positions where the generic premove hangs material, or give check to force a fallback. The engine depth slider controls how deeply it evaluates each scenario — lower depth gives you a bigger edge.
